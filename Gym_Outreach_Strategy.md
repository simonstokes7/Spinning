## 📊 Evaluation of Contact Methods

### 1. Try a Class First (⭐ Essential & High ROI)
* **Why:** You evaluate bike models (Schwinn, Keiser, Stages, RealRyder), sound system, rhythm vs. metric riding style, and member vibe.
* **Tactic:** Take a class, stick around for 2 minutes after class, introduce yourself to the instructor:  
  *"Great ride! I've been instructing since 1997 (previously at Lords and Next Gen for years), and I've recently refreshed my Spinning® cert and retired, so I'm looking to get back on local sub rosters. Who is the Group Fitness Manager here, and what's the best way to connect with them?"*

### 2. Personal Visit (⭐ Very Effective — Off-Peak Only)
* **Why:** Managers hire instructors based on warmth, energy, and presence.
* **Golden Timing Rule:** **NEVER drop in during peak hours** (6:00–8:30 AM or 5:00–7:30 PM). 
* **Best Window:** Tuesday through Thursday, **10:30 AM – 1:30 PM** or **2:30 PM – 4:00 PM**.
* **What to ask front desk:** *"Hi! Is the Group Fitness Manager / Group Ex Director in today?"* (Avoid asking for general management).

### 3. One-Page Resume (.docx or Print) (⭐ Mandatory)
* **Why:** GFMs scan resumes in <10 seconds. Keep it strictly to 1 page.
* **Must Include:**
  * Background spanning Glasgow (1997), Perth Fitness First, Newcastle, Lords Subiaco & Next Generation Kings Park.
  * Official Certifications (Spinning® 2026, CPR/AED, AusActive registration, Insurance).
  * Direct Link to your Live Class Builder/Profiles (https://simonstokes7.github.io/Spinning/).
  * Flexible Availability & Sub List Pitch.
